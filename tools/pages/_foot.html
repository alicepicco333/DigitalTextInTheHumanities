Words of Gender, the prototype of an archive of women authors.

Developed for the course Digital Text in the Humanities held by Professor Tiziana Mancinelli, MA Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge, University of Bologna, 2023.

Team: Nicole Liggeri, Chiara Parravicini, Lucia Bertoldini, Alice Picco.

Texts encoded in TEI P5 with linked open data. Source and XML on GitHub.
