How the words were tagged

For each concept that a word can name, the build script finds every occurrence of that word in the texts (by stem, in the language of each text). In 2023 the encoders tagged the passages that argue about a concept, and left many occurrences of its word untagged. In 2026 each of those 187 occurrences was read in context: the 128 that are an instance of the concept, as the alphabet of concepts defines it, are now tagged, as single words marked resp="#ed2026" in the TEI, apart from the encoders’ own tags. The other 59 are left untagged, each with its reason: grazia that is the grace of God, jeune (young) that only looks like jeûne (fasting), the honour of a knight rather than of a woman. The last column counts the opposite case, passages tagged in 2023 that never use the word, where the reading does work a search cannot.
